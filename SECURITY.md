# Security Policy

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to `bee-security@heossi.com`. Do not include secrets, customer data, or exploit details in a public issue.

Include the affected package or endpoint, observed and expected behavior, reproduction steps, impact, and a safe way to contact you. HEOSSI will acknowledge receipt, triage the report, coordinate remediation, and agree disclosure timing with the reporter.

Good-faith research that avoids privacy violations, service disruption, data destruction, social engineering, and unauthorized persistence is welcome. This policy does not authorize testing against other customers or exceeding your own account permissions.

For ordinary product support, use [bee.heossi.com/contact](https://bee.heossi.com/contact).

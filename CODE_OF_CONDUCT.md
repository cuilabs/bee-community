# Code of Conduct

Bee Public is a professional, inclusive developer community. Be respectful, constructive, technically honest, and mindful that participants have different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and restrict participation when conduct harms the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to `bee-support@heossi.com`. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and fairly.
